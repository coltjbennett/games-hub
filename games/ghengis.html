❓ How to Play

Placement: pick a unit and tap/drag on your side of the field. Units cost gold; walls and orders are cheap or free.
  • Draw Path – units placed near a path's start follow it, fighting as they go.
  • Target Flag – enemies inside its ring become priority targets.
  • Delete – brush away your own units, walls, paths and flags for a refund.
  • Wooden Walls – drag to build a palisade. They block movement and shots (cannons, trebuchets and siege towers fire over them). Blocked units chop walls down.
Support: Shamans heal, Throat Singers and Siege Towers rage-buff allies (faint circles show range while placing). Siege Towers also loose arrows at up to 7 targets at once.

Versus: split screen, two players can touch at once. Each player's units only follow their own paths and flags.

In battle (real-time orders):
  • Select: click or tap a unit, drag a box, double-tap for all of a type, Ctrl+A for everyone.
  • Orders: Move (M), Attack Area (T), Hold (H), Fall Back (F), Resume AI (R). Mouse: right-click the ground to move, an enemy to attack that group.
  • Take control: long-press (or hold the mouse on) one of your units. Move with WASD / arrows / the on-screen stick, attack with E / Enter / the ATTACK button, release with X or Esc.
  • In split screen, Player 1 uses WASD + E and Player 2 uses the arrow keys + Enter.
  • Ladder Carriers plant a ladder on the first wall, barricade, rock or water in their way (not trees); your whole team can then climb across it.
Camera: Space+drag rotate · Shift+drag pan · wheel zoom · two-finger drag/pinch on touch.
